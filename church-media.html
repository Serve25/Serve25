Straight answers

Questions we get
every week.

Anything not covered here, ask on the phone. We would rather talk you out of unnecessary work than bill you for it.

Do we have to buy new equipment?

Usually less than you think. Most rooms we walk into have adequate gear that was installed hastily. We tune what you own first and give you a ranked list of what is genuinely worth replacing.

Can you train our volunteers instead of doing it for us?

That is the preferred outcome. Every install ends with a training session and a written runbook, because the person running Sunday should not be a phone call away.

Do you work with small congregations?

Yes — a 45-seat chapel gets the same assessment as a 900-seat sanctuary. Small rooms are often easier and cheaper to fix well.
