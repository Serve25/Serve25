How it goes

Four steps, and none of them are a portal.

The same process whether you are calling about a server, a parent, or a sound board.

  1. 01

    You tell us what is going on

    A phone call or the form below. No intake portal, no discovery call about your discovery call. Describe the problem in your own words.

  2. 02

    We quote it before we drive

    A flat price or an hourly estimate with a ceiling, in writing. If the honest answer is that you do not need us, we will say so on the phone for free.

  3. 03

    We show up when we said

    A two-hour arrival window, not a workday. If we are running late you get a text before the window closes, not after.

  4. 04

    You get it in writing

    What we did, what we touched, what to watch. Families get visit notes the same evening; offices get a short service record.
